VTLOG Live PiP Bookmarklet

Opens a Chrome Document Picture-in-Picture window showing your live VTLOG job data.

Install

  1. Enter your Steam ID:
  2. Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  3. Drag this link to your bookmarks bar:

VTLOG Live PiP

Usage

  1. Navigate to your VTLOG company live page.
  2. Click the VTLOG Live PiP bookmark.
  3. A 1024×75 floating PiP window appears with your driver row.
  4. Live data syncs every second. Close the PiP window to stop.
Requirements: Chrome 116+ with Document Picture-in-Picture API. Enter your Steam ID above — the bookmarklet link updates automatically.
